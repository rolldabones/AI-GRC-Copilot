## 3. Stress-test preview (full version after intake)

These are the failure modes I will design against:
- **Stale register:** entries are never rescored after model changes or incidents. Fix: event-driven triggers from #18, #20 and #21.
- **Shadow AI:** vendor SaaS with embedded AI never reaches the register. Fix: procurement intake hook.
- **Unsupported residual scores:** residual risk is lowered without control test evidence. This is the most common audit finding.
- **Orphan risks:** entries with no inventory ID or with a departed owner.
- **Silent acceptance:** High risks accepted by email with no Decisions Log entry.

## 4. DECIDE: two choices needed before drafting

**Decision 1: Scoring model**

| Option | Design | Effort | Audit strength |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Strict** | 5x5 likelihood x impact. Impact scored separately across 5 dimensions (safety, regulatory, financial, people or fairness, operational). Inherent and residual scores, plus a target score. | High | Strongest |
| **Balanced** | 5x5. Impact equals the highest of the 5 dimensions. Inherent and residual scores. | Medium | Strong |
| **Fast** | 3x3 High/Medium/Low. Residual score only. | Low | Weak; likely to need rework |

**Decision 2: Register granularity**

| Option | Design | Tradeoff |
|---|---|---|
| **Strict** | One row per risk per AI system | Most precise; more rows to maintain |
| **Balanced** | Standard risk library (about 20 to 30 AI risk types) applied to each system, with system-specific scores | Consistent and reportable; needs library upkeep |
| **Fast** | Enterprise-level AI risk themes only | Easy to start; hides system-level exposure |

My recommendation, for you to decide: Balanced and Balanced. These suit a logistics firm with a mix of safety-relevant and back-office AI. If you operate safety-critical robotics or autonomous vehicles, consider Strict for Decision 1.
