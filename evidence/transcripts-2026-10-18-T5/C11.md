#### 2.8 Control steps (register operation)
1. Intake: a trigger (2.9) creates a draft entry in ServiceNow within 5 business days
2. Assessment: a workshop with the risk owner, control owner and Risk Analyst scores inherent risk and documents the rationale
3. Control mapping: controls are linked with evidence. Residual risk is scored under the "no evidence, no credit" rule.
4. Thresholding: the band is applied, the override is checked and escalation is triggered
5. Treatment: actions are logged with owners and dates. Model changes go in Jira and everything else in ServiceNow.
6. Decision: acceptance, avoidance or a go-live gate decision is recorded in #23
7. Monitoring: KRIs are reviewed at the cadence for the band
8. Review: the next review date is enforced and overdue entries are flagged automatically
9. Closure: only when the system is retired or the risk no longer applies, with evidence. Risk Lead approves.

### Pack 7: Working habits and review gates

**Evidence-first habits**
- No score moves down without a link to evidence in SharePoint or ServiceNow
- The rationale is written at the time of scoring, not reconstructed later
- Vendor claims count as evidence only when documented and reviewed

**Stop rules (work halts and escalates)**
- Personal data would be processed without a signed-off DPIA
- A vendor would process data before a signed contract with minimum clauses
- Auto-rejection is found enabled
- Selection rate ratio breaches its threshold: suspend scoring visibility pending review
- An undisclosed vendor model change is detected
- A Critical risk has no treatment plan after 5 business days

**Human review checklist (before any entry moves to "Accepted" or "Closed")**
- [ ] Owner confirmed in writing
- [ ] Evidence opened and checked, not just linked
- [ ] Override rule applied correctly
- [ ] Approver matches the band
- [ ] Decision recorded in #23

**Failure patterns to watch**
- Risk owners are all "Risk team": accountability drifts away from the business
- Scores fall after a vendor sales call with no documents
- The register is updated only before Steering Group meetings
- "INV-PENDING" entries become permanent
- High recruiter agreement with rankings is read as accuracy when it may be rubber-stamping
- Acceptances never expire
