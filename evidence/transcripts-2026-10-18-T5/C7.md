# Pack Part 1: One-Page Executive Summary

**What this is:** A single plan for handling failures, harms and breaches caused by or involving Harrowgate's five AI systems. It runs inside the existing ServiceNow incident process.

**Why it matters:**
- **Safety:** Vigilo fatigue alerts and route optimisation directly affect road safety.
- **Privacy:** Vigilo processes in-cab video of drivers. The chatbot and invoice extraction handle customer and supplier data.
- **Money and contracts:** Invoice extraction errors and chatbot commitments create financial and contractual exposure.
- **Two jurisdictions:** UK and German data protection and employee-relations expectations apply. Legal confirms the specifics.

**How it works:**
1. Anyone can report a suspected AI incident to the IT Service Desk or through ServiceNow.
2. The AI Incident Manager classifies it as Sev1 (Critical) to Sev4 (Low or near miss).
3. The System Owner contains it, using a pre-agreed fallback for each system.
4. Escalation triggers fire automatically by severity and type: personal data goes to Legal and the DPO, safety goes to Fleet Safety, drivers go to HR, Sev1 goes to the COO.
5. Only Legal decides whether to contact a regulator. Only the Comms Lead, with Legal approval, speaks externally.
6. Sev1 and Sev2 incidents receive a postmortem within 10 business days. Corrective and preventive actions (CAPA) are tracked to closure in ServiceNow.

- Sponsor contract remediation for the three vendors. Incident readiness is **Blocked** until notification clauses are evidenced.

# Pack Part 7: Working Habits and Review Gates

**Evidence-first habits**
- If it is not in ServiceNow, it did not happen. Log first, then act. For Sev1, act and log within 30 minutes.
- Keep the awareness time separate from the ticket time.
- Preserve before you fix.
- Facts in tickets, opinions to Legal.

**Stop rules**
- Stop and escalate to Legal before any regulator, media or liability-related vendor contact.
- Stop and escalate to the Fleet Safety Lead before disabling Vigilo or making route changes with safety implications.
- Stop if a fix requires deleting personal data or logs. Get DPO or Legal approval first.
- Stop restoring a Sev1 system without COO approval.

**Human review checklist (before closure)**
- [ ] Severity rationale matches the matrix
- [ ] All triggered escalations are timestamped
- [ ] Notification decision recorded (Sev1 and Sev2)
- [ ] Containment and restore approvals recorded
- [ ] Communications approved by Legal
