## 3. STRESS-TEST (preliminary, refined once I have your answers)

| Lens | Likely gap to design for |
|---|---|
| **Operational failure** | AI harms often show up slowly (bias, drift, wrong advice) and never trigger an outage alert, so nobody declares an incident. Containment then stalls because no one knows who can pull the plug or what the manual fallback is |
| **Auditor asks** | "Show me every AI incident in the last 12 months, its severity rationale, timeline, approvals and CAPA closure evidence." The gap is usually incidents handled in chat with no ticket |
| **Regulator asks** | "When did you become aware, who decided whether to notify and on what basis?" The "awareness" timestamp and the notification decision record must both be logged |
| **Disclosure risk** | Engineers speculating on cause in widely shared channels. Uncontrolled statements to customers before a Legal gate |
| **Vendor failure** | A vendor model changes or fails silently, the vendor has no duty to notify you and you cannot get logs |
| **Monitoring blind spots** | Customer complaints, front-line staff reports and social media are not wired into intake |
| **Simplest control that works** | One intake channel, one ticket type ("AI Incident") with mandatory fields, a severity matrix with objective triggers and a named incident commander per severity |
| **Must be logged** | Detection time, awareness time, declaration, severity and its rationale, affected system and version, containment actions and approver, notification decisions, comms sent, root cause, CAPA owners and due dates, closure sign-off |

## 4. DECIDE (please pick one option for each)

### Decision A: Integration model

| Option | Description | Tradeoff |
|---|---|---|
| **Strict** | Standalone AI incident process with its own incident commander pool and an AI Incident Review Board | Strong AI expertise. Higher overhead and a risk of parallel processes |
| **Balanced** (my default suggestion) | AI incidents run through the existing incident process, with an AI annex: AI-specific triggers, an AI incident lead role and mandatory Legal/Privacy/Fairness review gates for higher severities | Reuses existing muscle. Depends on the existing process being sound |
| **Fast** | Existing incident process plus an "AI" tag and a short checklist | Quick to launch. Weak on harm-to-people incidents and audit evidence |

| Option | Description |
