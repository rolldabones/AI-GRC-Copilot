# 1. One-Page Executive Summary

**What this is:** The plan that tells Harrowgate when its five AI systems are underperforming or failing, who must act and how fast, and what evidence proves it.

**Why it matters**
- Driver-fatigue alerts are a safety and employee-privacy system. A silent failure could mean missed fatigue events. Frequent false alerts could erode driver trust or create unfair treatment.
- Route optimization and demand forecasting drive daily operations. Degradation shows up as late deliveries and wasted capacity before anyone notices the model.
- The chatbot and invoice extraction run on vendor models that can change without Harrowgate's involvement.

**How it works**
1. Each system is assigned a provisional tier: Tier 1 Vigilo; Tier 2 route optimization, chatbot and invoice extraction; Tier 3 demand forecasting.
2. Each system has a monitoring spec covering availability, accuracy, drift, human oversight signals and, for vendors, contract evidence.
3. Datadog raises alerts to the system owner. Breaches become ServiceNow tickets. Serious breaches escalate to the risk team and COO and hand off to the Incident Plan (#18).
4. Thresholds are set after a 30-day baseline and approved. Outage, no-data and safety-constraint alerts are live from day one.
5. Tier 1 gets a weekly review. The steering group gets a monthly report.

**Decisions needed from the COO**
- Confirm the five balanced-option decisions (D-21-01 to D-21-05)
- Name a system owner for each of the five systems
- Confirm whether Vigilo alerts are ever used in HR or disciplinary decisions
- Confirm evidence retention period

**Top risks if not adopted:** undetected safety system failure, unapproved vendor model changes, no audit trail, privacy complaints from drivers.

**Headline status:** Vigilo monitoring is **Blocked** pending vendor evidence and DPIA confirmation (Section 2.7). The other systems can start under this plan once owners are named.

# 7. Working Habits and Review Gates

**Evidence-first habits**
- No threshold exists unless it is in the Threshold Register with an approver
- No breach is handled in chat or email alone. If it is not in ServiceNow, it did not happen.
- Export Datadog evidence monthly. Do not rely on platform retention.
- Log known business changes before they show up as drift

**Stop rules**
- Stop onboarding a system if it has no named system owner
- Stop signing off Tier 1 monitoring if vendor evidence is "Blocked" or the DPIA is not confirmed
- Stop and escalate if a hard-constraint violation reaches dispatch
- Stop new driver-level or agent-level reporting until the DPIA review and Legal review are complete
- Stop loosening thresholds during an open breach without the tier approver

**Human review checklist (monthly, AI Risk Lead)**
- [ ] Every metric has a no-data monitor
- [ ] Threshold Register reconciles to Datadog
- [ ] All Sev 1 and Sev 2 tickets have escalation timestamps
- [ ] Vendor reports received or chased
- [ ] QA samples completed by approved roles
- [ ] Vigilo subgroup results reviewed and any gaps ticketed
- [ ] Driver and customer challenges counted from #24
- [ ] Expiring exceptions listed
- [ ] Open items progressed

**Common failure patterns to watch for**
- Dashboards nobody opens
- Alerts routed to a shared inbox with no owner
- Baselines set during an abnormal period (peak season, outage)
- Vendor "all green" reports never checked against Harrowgate sampling
- Monitoring of employees expanding without privacy review
- Override rates ignored, even though they are often the earliest signal of quality loss
