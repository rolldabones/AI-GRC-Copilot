## 1. Executive Summary (One Page)

**What this is:** The single, repeatable process Harrowgate Freight uses when any regulator in the UK or Germany asks about, inspects or must be told about its AI systems.

**Why it matters:**
- Five AI systems are in use. Three come from vendors (Quillon AI, Ledgerline and Vigilo). One (Vigilo) records in-cab video of drivers. That combination of employee monitoring, vendor dependency and two jurisdictions is where regulator questions are most likely.
- Today there is no defined intake, no regulator log and no pre-approved evidence. A regulator request could reach a depot, a vendor or a customer-service agent and go unrecorded.

**How it works:**
1. **One door in:** All regulator contact goes to a single monitored mailbox and becomes a ServiceNow Regulatory Matter within 1 business day.
2. **One owner:** Legal owns every matter. A Regulatory Liaison (Risk team) runs logistics and deadlines.
3. **One gate:** No substantive statement leaves Harrowgate without Legal approval recorded in ServiceNow.
4. **One evidence base:** Pre-built doc packs for the two highest-sensitivity systems (Vigilo and chatbot). Other systems use a standard index assembled within 5 business days.
5. **One escalation path:** Liaison, then Head of Legal, then COO, then AI Steering Group (or the Board for Tier 1 matters, per open item).

**Decisions the COO is asked to approve:** D-29-01 to D-29-06 (Section 0.4).

**Top risks if not adopted:** missed statutory deadlines, inconsistent statements across countries, inability to produce vendor evidence and unmanaged scrutiny of driver video monitoring.

**Effort:** Designed for a 2-person risk team. Heaviest work is the first 60 days (Vigilo and chatbot doc packs plus vendor clause checks). Effort estimate in hours is [TBD].

**Key open items:** DPO appointment, German works council status, named Legal lead, Vigilo use in employment decisions, vendor contract cooperation terms and the regulator map confirmation by Legal.

## 7. Working Habits and Review Gates

### 7.1 Evidence-First Habits
- No fact goes to a regulator unless it traces to an indexed, attested source.
- "Unknown" is an acceptable answer internally. Guessing is not.
- Log first, then act. A contact that is not in ServiceNow does not exist for KPI and audit purposes.
- One statement of record per matter. Update it rather than creating parallel versions.

### 7.2 Stop Rules
- Stop if G3 or G4 approval is missing. Escalate rather than send.
- Stop if a system owner cannot attest a fact. Mark it Not available.
- Stop if a vendor-sourced fact is not confirmed in writing by the vendor.
- Stop if personal data appears in a draft that Legal has not cleared.
- Stop and call Legal if anyone suggests editing, deleting or backdating a record.

### 7.3 Human Review Checklist (Before Any Release)
- [ ] Do we know exactly what the regulator asked?
- [ ] Does every statement have a source?
- [ ] Have system owners attested?
- [ ] Is this consistent with what we told any regulator before? (Check prior matters for the same system)
- [ ] Are commitments realistic for a 2-person risk team?
- [ ] Is the right approver recorded?

### 7.4 Known Failure Patterns

| Pattern | Early warning sign | Countermeasure |
|---|---|---|
| Contact never reaches intake | Quarterly reconciliation finds gaps. Regulator chases an unknown request | Awareness campaign (#27). Training in onboarding (#26). Depot and Germany site briefings |
