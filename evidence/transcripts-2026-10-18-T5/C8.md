**Score = Likelihood x Overall impact.**

| Band | Score range |
|---|---|
| Low | 1 to 4 |
| Medium | 5 to 9 |
| High | 10 to 15 |
| Critical | 16 to 25 |

**Override rule (proposed):** any risk with a Safety and people impact of 5 is at least High, regardless of likelihood.

**Inherent vs residual**
- **Inherent:** the score assuming no controls.
- **Residual:** the score after existing controls, credited only if the evidence field links to proof that the control operated in the last review period.
- Without evidence, residual equals inherent.

## Part 7: Working Habits and Review Gates

**Evidence-first habits**
- No evidence, no residual credit.
- Every score change carries a one-line rationale.
- Business owns risks. The risk team owns the method and never becomes the default owner.
- Score in short workshops by system, not by email chains.

**Stop rules (do not proceed; escalate to risk team lead)**
- The risk owner is unnamed.
- A residual score is lower than inherent and the evidence is not verified.
- Personal data is involved and there is no DPIA reference or "DPIA required" flag.
- Any AI output is used in an employment or disciplinary decision without human oversight, adverse impact analysis and an appeal route. This escalates to the steering group.
- An in-house model release has no completed risk check in Jira.
- An acceptance has expired or the approver is below the authority for the band.

**Human review checklist (before steering group)**
- [ ] All High and Critical entries reviewed within cadence
- [ ] Evidence sample of 20% opened and confirmed
- [ ] New trigger events reflected
- [ ] Acceptances within authority and not expired
- [ ] KPIs calculated from the ServiceNow export, not edited by hand

**Failure patterns to watch**
- Register written once and never reviewed. Watch the overdue KPI.
- Every risk scored Medium to avoid escalation. Analyst challenges clusters.
- Controls listed that nobody operates.
- Vendor risks owned by Legal instead of the business user.
- Datadog monitoring uptime only, not model quality.
- Workshops slipping because the 2-person team lacks capacity. Protect a fixed monthly slot.
