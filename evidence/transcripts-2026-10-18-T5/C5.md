# THE SPELLBOOK PACK

## 1. One-Page Executive Summary

**What this is:** A single procedure for changing any of Harrowgate's 5 AI systems safely, whether the change is made by our engineers or by a vendor.

**Why it matters:**
- Two systems affect people directly: Vigilo monitors drivers on camera; the Quillon chatbot speaks to customers.
- One system affects money: Ledgerline extracts invoice data.
- Two systems drive operations: route optimisation and demand forecasting.
- An uncontrolled change in any of these can harm drivers, customers, cash or service, and we would be unable to prove what changed or why.

**How it works:**
1. Every change gets a Jira ticket.
2. The ticket assigns a tier: Minor, Standard, Major or Emergency.
3. Approval scales with tier: model owner (Minor), model owner plus risk team (Standard), COO or AI Steering Group (Major).
4. Privacy screening runs on every ticket. Personal data changes trigger a DPIA.
5. Nothing deploys without passing tests, a rollback plan and approval.
6. Datadog marks every deployment. Risk reconciles deployments against approvals monthly.
7. Vendor changes follow the same path, but our contracts may not yet require vendors to tell us about changes. This path is **Blocked** until Legal confirms the clauses.

**Decisions for the COO:**
- Ratify 7 design choices (DL-020-01 to 07).
- Name model owners for 5 systems and a Risk Lead.
- Direct Legal to review 3 vendor contracts for change-notice clauses.

**Key risks today:** no named model owners; unknown vendor change clauses; unknown use of Vigilo alerts in HR decisions; unknown works council status in Germany.

**Headline KPIs:** 100% of Tier 2 and 3 changes approved before deployment; 0 unreconciled deployments; under 10% of changes classed Emergency (proposed targets, to be ratified).

### 5.3 Emergency path
1. Model Owner contains issue; calls COO or delegate for verbal approval.
2. Deploy fix or roll back; Datadog marker with "EMERGENCY" tag.
3. Raise Jira ticket within 4 hours, citing incident number from ServiceNow.
4. Risk Lead runs retrospective review within 2 business days: was it a genuine emergency; were tests adequate; is a follow-up Tier 2 or 3 change needed.
5. Report at next steering group.

### 5.4 Vendor-detected path
1. Datadog drift alert or user report suggests unannounced vendor change.
2. Vendor Owner raises ticket (source: Vendor-detected) and contacts vendor in writing within 1 business day.
3. Model Owner assesses impact; if harm, open incident under #18.
4. Risk Lead decides: accept (retrospective Tier 2), require vendor rollback or suspend feature (COO approval).
5. Log as contract issue for Legal.

## 7. Working Habits and Review Gates

**Evidence-first habits**
- If it is not in Jira, it did not happen.
- Attach evidence when the step happens, not at closure.
- Name a person, not a team, in every approval field.
- Link, do not copy: tickets link to DPIAs, model cards and incidents.

**Stop rules (any one halts deployment)**
1. Jira ticket not in "Approved" status.
2. Approval timestamp missing or after deploy.
3. Privacy screening has an unresolved "Yes" or "Unknown".
4. Tier 2 or 3 change with no tested rollback plan (in-house).
5. Vigilo change without adverse impact analysis and Legal gate.
6. Test results fail a threshold with no approved exception.
7. Vendor change with Blocked status and no compensating monitor in place.

**Human review checklist (approver)**
- [ ] I understand what changes and who is affected
- [ ] Tier matches the criteria
- [ ] Tests passed against stated thresholds
- [ ] Rollback is realistic and owned
- [ ] Privacy and legal gates are closed
- [ ] Monitoring window and owner are set
- [ ] I am the correct approver for this tier

**Common failure patterns**
- "Just a config change": thresholds and prompts are model changes.
- Vendor changes treated as outside our control: we remain accountable for outcomes.
- Emergency label used to skip review.
- Model card not updated, so documentation drifts from production.
- Reconciliation skipped in busy months: this is the only control that catches unapproved changes.
