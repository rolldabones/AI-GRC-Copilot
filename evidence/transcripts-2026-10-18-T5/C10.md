### A2.2 Scope and definition of "AI system"

**In scope:** any system that uses machine learning, statistical models or generative AI to produce predictions, recommendations, classifications, content or decisions. This includes:
- Systems built in-house
- Vendor products
- AI features embedded in existing SaaS tools
- GenAI tools used by staff for work purposes

**Out of scope:** fixed rule-based logic with no learned component, such as static spreadsheet formulas. When in doubt, register the system and let Risk decide.

**Note:** this is an operational definition. Legal to confirm alignment with UK and EU definitions (OI-20, jurisdiction UK and DE, uncertainty: medium).

### A2.3 Classification method

**Step 1: Screening questionnaire** (A4.2). The System Owner completes it and Risk validates it.

**Step 2: Tier triggers.** The highest triggered tier applies.

| Tier | Triggers (any one is sufficient) |
|---|---|
| **Prohibited** | Legal determines the use is prohibited under applicable law in the UK or Germany. Or the use breaches the Code of Conduct (#3), for example inferring emotions of staff or covert monitoring of workers. |
| **High** | (a) Makes or materially informs decisions about individuals' work allocation, monitoring, performance, discipline or employment. (b) Continuous monitoring of individuals by video, audio or location. (c) Processes special category or biometric data. (d) Safety-critical: failure or error could contribute to physical harm. (e) Fully automated decision with legal or similarly significant effect on a person. (f) Legal flags the use as likely high-risk under applicable AI law. |
| **Medium** | (a) Processes personal data and no High trigger applies. (b) Outputs reach customers or the public directly. (c) Outputs feed financial, contractual or billing records. (d) Generates content used externally. |
| **Low** | None of the above. Internal, advisory, aggregated or non-personal data, and a human reviews before use. |

**Step 3: Supplementary scores (1 to 4).** These set review priority within a tier and provide a cross-check.

| Dimension | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Impact on people | None | Minor inconvenience | Affects service, cost or workload of individuals | Affects safety, employment, legal rights or livelihood |
| Data sensitivity | No personal data | Business contact data | Personal data of customers or staff | Special category, biometric, video of individuals or location tracking |
| Decision autonomy | Insight only | Recommendation, human decides | Default action, human can override | Fully automated |
| Operational criticality | Nice to have | Degraded service if down | Core process disrupted | Safety, legal or major revenue impact |

**Threshold rules:**
- Any score of 4 in Impact, Data sensitivity or Criticality means the tier is at least High.
- Any score of 3 means the tier is at least Medium.
- If triggers and scores disagree, the higher tier applies.

**Step 4: Uncertainty rule (D-01).** If a trigger answer is "Unknown", classify one tier higher until it is resolved. Record an open item with a due date of 30 days or less.

**Step 5: Confirmation.**
- Risk Lead confirms Low and Medium.
- Steering group confirms High and Prohibited, recorded in the minutes and the Decisions Log.

### C2.3 Materiality

Otherwise the change is **non-material**, for example a performance-neutral retrain on the same data schema within tolerance.

If materiality is uncertain, treat the change as material.
