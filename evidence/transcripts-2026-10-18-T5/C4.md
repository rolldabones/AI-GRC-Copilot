### 1. Objective
Ensure that Harrowgate uses AI safely, lawfully and fairly. Ensure that every AI system has a named owner, a risk tier, the controls that tier requires and evidence that proves those controls ran.

### 5. Requirements

Each requirement is identified as **AIP-xx** for testing and audit.

#### 5.1 Inventory
- **AIP-01:** No AI system may be used in production unless it has an inventory record in ServiceNow. *Evidence:* inventory record with owner, tier, vendor, data types and status.
- **AIP-02:** AI owners confirm their inventory record is accurate every quarter. *Evidence:* attestation timestamp in ServiceNow.

#### 5.2 Risk Tiering
- **AIP-03:** Every AI system is assigned a tier at intake using the criteria below. The tier is reassessed on material change. *Evidence:* tier decision recorded in ServiceNow with rationale and assessor.

| Tier | Criteria (any one is enough) |
|---|---|
| Tier 1 High | Processes video, audio or biometric data of identifiable people. Processes special category personal data. Informs adverse decisions about individuals. Affects physical safety. Customer-facing with no human review and significant effect on the customer. |
| Tier 2 Medium | Processes personal data not covered by Tier 1. Customer-facing with human fallback. Feeds financial, contractual or regulatory records. |
| Tier 3 Low | Internal use only. No personal data. Output reviewed by a human before use. |

#### 5.3 Approval Gates

| Gate | Tier 1 | Tier 2 | Tier 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Intake ticket (ServiceNow) | Required | Required | Required |
| Risk assessment (per #5 Framework) | Full | Standard | Self-assessment |
| DPIA screening | Required | Required | Required if personal data appears |
| Full DPIA | Required if personal data | If screening triggers | If screening triggers |
| Legal review | Required | Required for vendor AI | Required for vendor AI |
| Pre-deployment testing (incl. bias where people affected) | Required, reviewed by Risk | Required, sampled by Risk | Owner-attested |
| Approver | Steering group | Head of Risk | AI owner |

- **AIP-04:** No system goes live until all gates for its tier are complete. *Evidence:* ServiceNow approval record linking each gate artifact.

#### 5.4 Data and Privacy
- **AIP-05:** DPIA screening is completed for every AI system. A full DPIA is required where any trigger applies: monitoring of employees; video, audio or biometric data; large-scale customer personal data; automated decisions with significant effect; new technology applied to personal data; transfers outside the UK or EEA. *Evidence:* screening form and DPIA in SharePoint, signed off by the data protection lead.
- **AIP-06:** Personal data used by AI is limited to what is necessary for the stated purpose. A retention period is defined for each system. *Evidence:* data map and retention entry per system.
- **AIP-07:** Data subject rights (access, correction, objection, erasure where applicable) are supported for AI-processed data. Requests are logged and answered within legal timelines [Legal to confirm]. *Evidence:* request log in ServiceNow.
- **AIP-08:** For any AI processing employee data in Germany, Legal confirms whether employee representative consultation is required before deployment or change [OI-03]. *Evidence:* Legal memo in SharePoint.

#### 5.5 Human Oversight, Adverse Impact and Appeals
- **AIP-09:** No adverse decision about an employee or customer may be based solely on AI output. A competent, trained person must review the output and record their decision. *Evidence:* review record per decision (system log or ServiceNow).
- **AIP-10:** Tier 1 systems affecting individuals require an adverse impact analysis before go-live and annually after that. The analysis tests error and false-alert rates across relevant groups and conditions. For Vigilo examples include eyewear, lighting, skin tone, age band and shift pattern [final groups TBD with Legal and HR]. *Evidence:* test report with thresholds and sign-off.
- **AIP-11:** Individuals affected by an AI-informed decision can challenge it through a documented appeal route. The appeal is reviewed by a person not involved in the original decision. *Evidence:* appeals log with outcomes and turnaround times.

#### 5.6 Vendors and Third Parties
- **AIP-12:** Vendor AI passes due diligence under #19 Vendor Policy before contract signature or renewal. *Evidence:* due diligence record in ServiceNow.
- **AIP-13:** AI vendor contracts include, at minimum:
  - data processing terms, data location and sub-processor list
  - no use of Harrowgate data to train vendor models without written consent
  - advance notice of material model changes [notice period TBD]
  - security incident notice within [TBD] hours
  - audit and information rights
  - performance and accuracy reporting
  - exit, data return and deletion
  - liability allocation [Legal to set]

  *Evidence:* Legal clause checklist attached to the contract record.
- **AIP-14:** If any minimum clause or evidence is missing, the vendor status is **Blocked** for new scope or renewal until it is fixed or an exception is approved. *Evidence:* status field in ServiceNow.

#### 5.7 Change Management
- **AIP-15:** Material changes to in-house models are raised and approved in Jira under #20 Change Management. Tier 1 changes need steering group approval. Tier 2 changes need Head of Risk approval. *Evidence:* Jira ticket with approval, test results and rollback plan.
- **AIP-16:** Vendor model change notices are logged in Jira and assessed for tier impact. *Evidence:* Jira ticket per notice.

#### 5.8 Monitoring
- **AIP-17:** Each in-house system has defined performance and drift metrics, thresholds and alert routing in Datadog before go-live. *Evidence:* Datadog monitor configuration and owner mapping.
- **AIP-18:** Vendor system performance is reviewed against contract reports at least quarterly for Tier 1 and 2. *Evidence:* review record in ServiceNow.

#### 5.9 Incidents
- **AIP-19:** AI incidents are logged in ServiceNow and handled under #18 Incident Response Plan. An AI incident means harmful, unlawful, biased, unsafe or materially wrong output, a data breach or vendor failure. Tier 1 incidents are escalated to the COO and Legal within [TBD] hours. *Evidence:* incident record with timeline, classification and corrective actions.
- **AIP-20:** Only Legal decides on and makes contact with regulators. *Evidence:* Legal decision note in the incident record.

#### 5.10 Transparency
- **AIP-21:** Customers interacting with AI, such as the Quillon chatbot, are told they are interacting with AI and are offered a route to a human. *Evidence:* screenshot of the disclosure and escalation path, retested on each release.
- **AIP-22:** Employees subject to AI monitoring, such as Vigilo, receive clear written notice of what is captured, why, how long it is kept, who sees it and how to challenge outcomes. *Evidence:* notice version and distribution record.

#### 5.11 Training
- **AIP-23:** AI owners, technical owners, reviewers under AIP-09 and steering group members complete role training under #26 before taking up the role and annually after that. *Evidence:* training completion records [system TBD, OI-07].

#### 5.12 Records and Retirement
- **AIP-24:** Evidence is retained per the Harrowgate retention schedule [TBD, OI-08].
- **AIP-25:** Retired systems are marked as retired in the inventory. Their data is deleted or archived per the DPIA, and vendor deletion is confirmed. *Evidence:* retirement checklist and vendor deletion certificate.

#### 5.13 Transition for Live Systems
- **AIP-26:** All 5 live systems complete intake and tiering within 30 days of policy approval. Tier 1 gates must be complete within 60 days and all gates within 90 days. Gaps beyond those dates need an exception. *Evidence:* transition tracker in ServiceNow.
