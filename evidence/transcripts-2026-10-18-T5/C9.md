# SECTION 1. One-Page Executive Summary

**What it is:** The AI Decisions Log is the single record of material AI decisions at Harrowgate Freight. It answers 5 questions for any decision: what, who, on what authority, on what evidence and until when.

**Why it matters:**
- We run 5 AI systems. Three come from vendors (Quillon AI, Ledgerline and Vigilo), and one, Vigilo, processes in-cab video of our drivers.
- Without a log, we cannot prove to auditors, customers, data protection authorities or works councils how and why we approved these systems.
- The log turns the monthly Steering Group from discussion into accountable, evidenced decisions.

**How it works:**
- **System of record:** ServiceNow, alongside the existing risk register. Evidence stays where it lives (Jira, SharePoint, Datadog, ServiceNow) and is linked.
- **Three decision tiers:** system owners decide low-impact matters. The Risk lead must concur on medium-impact matters. The Steering Group and COO decide high-impact matters, including new AI systems, new vendors, high risks and anything affecting drivers' or customers' rights.
- **Integrity:** approved entries are never edited, only superseded. Monthly snapshots go to a restricted SharePoint library.
- **Completeness:** each month, Risk reconciles Jira changes, risk register acceptances and Steering Group minutes against the log.
- **Emergency path:** the COO can decide immediately. The decision is logged within 1 business day and ratified within 10.

**Key risks if not adopted:** Vigilo and chatbot decisions cannot be evidenced. Accepted risks never expire. Vendor go-lives may lack minimum contract protections.

2. Onboarding, renewal with scope change, material contract change or exit of an AI vendor.
3. Any risk acceptance, at any rating, for an AI risk in the ServiceNow risk register.
4. Any exception to an AI policy or standard.
5. A material model change as defined in #20. Until #20 exists, this means retraining, new data source, new feature, new model version from a vendor or threshold change.
6. New or changed processing of personal data by an AI system, including retention changes.
7. Any decision on how AI outputs affect people's rights, pay, employment, discipline, customer commitments or pricing. Examples: use of Vigilo alerts in HR processes, or chatbot authority to make customer commitments.
8. Acceptance of a monitoring threshold breach in Datadog without remediation.
9. Containment, notification or closure decisions during an AI incident under #18.

# SECTION 7. Working Habits and Review Gates

**Evidence-first habits:**
- Link evidence before submitting, not after approval.
- Write the decision in one sentence that someone outside the team could test.
- Record dissent. If the Risk lead or Legal disagreed, say so in the rationale.
- Put a date on every risk acceptance and exception. If no one wants to set a date, that is a signal the decision is not real.

**Stop rules (approval is blocked if any apply):**
1. No approver authority reference.
2. Tier 2 or 3 with no evidence links.
3. Personal data = Y with no DPIA reference or documented DPO screening.
4. Rights impact = Y with no human oversight reference, adverse impact reference and appeal route.
5. Vendor decision with minimum clauses "Not met" and no Tier 3 risk acceptance.
6. COO-sponsored decision with the COO as sole approver.
7. Jira material change moving to deploy with no Decision ID.
8. AI vendor contract presented for signature with no Decision ID.

**Human review checklist (Risk lead, Tier 2 and 3):**
- [ ] Tier is correct and the rationale is stated
- [ ] Options are genuine, including "do nothing"
- [ ] Decision text is specific and testable
- [ ] Evidence links open and support the rationale
- [ ] Residual risk matches the risk register
- [ ] Expiry or review date set
- [ ] DPIA, vendor and rights fields complete where required
- [ ] No personal data in free text
- [ ] Follow-up actions created with owners and dates

**Common failure patterns:**
| Pattern | Signal | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Rubber-stamp entries | Identical rationale text across entries | Reviewer rejects. Require system-specific rationale. |
| Logging after the fact | Retrospective flag rate above 20% | Raise at Steering Group. Add Jira and Procurement gates. |
| Perpetual risk acceptances | Same risk renewed 3 or more times | Escalate to Tier 3 with a remediation plan |
| Evidence rot | Broken SharePoint links | Monthly sample. Use stable document IDs. |
| Steering Group decides without entry | Minutes lack Decision IDs | Secretary uses decision ID column in minutes template |
| Shadow decisions in vendor portals | Vendor settings changed by admins | Treat vendor configuration changes as material changes under #20 |
| Risk team overload | Tier 2 backlog above 10 entries | Move Tier 2 review to a fixed weekly slot. Review tier thresholds. |
